# Art Direction System: "Always Visible. Always Useful."

**Core idea**  
The 7.5-inch smart workspace display is positioned as a calm, always-present information surface that keeps what matters most visible — without competing with the main computer screen. Each visual shows a clear reason to care: personal productivity, business metrics, shared spaces, hospitality, and deep focus.

**Visual principles**
- Premium, cinematic lighting and materials
- Product always sharp, correctly proportioned and branded “Async Labs”
- Strong side-panel marketing messaging that reinforces the benefit
- Consistent modern dark/light contrast language across the set
- Real-world contexts that feel desirable and commercially relevant

---

## Visual 01 — Hero
**File:** screenshots/01.png  
**Context:** Website hero / primary product marketing  
**Caption / Intent:**  
“Your Day, Always Visible.”  
A clean, premium desk hero that immediately communicates the core value: your schedule, focus goal and daily context stay in sight so you stay organized and in control. Soft lighting and minimal props keep the product as the hero.

## Visual 02 — Startup Command Center
**File:** screenshots/02.png  
**Context:** Founder / startup / business metrics marketing  
**Caption / Intent:**  
“Your business pulse. Always in sight.”  
Shows the display as a live business dashboard for founders and operators — revenue, orders, customers, monthly goal and top priorities — all visible without switching tabs. Positions the product as a decision-making tool, not just a calendar.

## Visual 03 — Meeting Room Display
**File:** screenshots/03.png  
**Context:** Office / facilities / shared space marketing  
**Caption / Intent:**  
“Know Before You Walk In.”  
Wall-mounted outside a meeting room, the display shows real-time availability, next meeting, and calendar sync. Demonstrates the wall-mount flexibility and team utility in a professional corporate environment.

## Visual 04 — Restaurant Display
**File:** screenshots/04.png  
**Context:** Hospitality / restaurant / retail marketing  
**Caption / Intent:**  
“Update the moment. Delight every guest.”  
An original and commercially strong use case. The display sits on a restaurant counter showing today’s special with photo, price and availability. Communicates easy remote updates, visual impact, and low-power reliability for hospitality environments.

## Visual 05 — Focus Mode Display
**File:** screenshots/05.png  
**Context:** Productivity / deep work / personal marketing  
**Caption / Intent:**  
“What matters. Nothing else.”  
A focused deep-work session view with countdown timer, session goal, priorities and notifications off. Positions the product as a tool that protects attention and supports high-quality work sessions.

---

**Why this set works together**  
The five visuals form a coherent system that moves from personal productivity → business metrics → shared spaces → hospitality → deep focus. Each image has strong commercial messaging, keeps the product recognizable, and shows clear reasons to care. The consistent treatment of lighting, framing and side-panel copy creates a unified brand feeling.
